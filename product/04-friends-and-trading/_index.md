# Friends and trading

## Why
Collecting is more fun together. Friends want to compare collections and swap spare cards fairly.

## Capabilities
None written yet.

## Acceptance
All points are *(proposed)* until agreed.

- Two users become friends only if both agree, and either can end it.
- A friend can look at a collection but never change it, and the owner can hide it.
- The app suggests swaps of spare cards for missing ones that are fair by rarity.
- Nothing moves without the other side accepting, and no card is lost or duplicated.
- Both sides can look up past trades.

## Open questions
- Is a trade the record of a real swap between people who meet?
- How do users find friends? Decided under [Safe for children](../01-safe-for-children/_index.md).
