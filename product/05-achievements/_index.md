# Achievements

## Why
Rewards make collecting fun beyond keeping a list: users earn achievements, experience points and
ranks for what they collect and do.

## Capabilities
None written yet.

## Acceptance
All points are *(proposed)* until agreed.

- A user sees their rank, experience points and achievements, including what is still locked and
  how far along they are.
- A new achievement, rank or card is celebrated when it happens.
- Rewards cannot be farmed with duplicate photos.
- A child can understand how to earn the next reward.
- Rewards do not pressure children to come back.

## Open questions
- What should achievements encourage: collecting, careful checking, trading?
- One achievement needs a `legendary` card, and the catalog has none yet. Keep it visible?
- No spec describes the achievement list and the rank ladder as a whole.
