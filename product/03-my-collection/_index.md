# My collection

## Why
Collectors want to know what they have, what is missing and what is spare, without sorting
through piles and folders.

## Capabilities
None written yet.

## Acceptance
All points are *(proposed)* until agreed.

- For every series, a user sees which cards they own, which are missing and which are spare.
- A user can browse a series as pictures and find a single card by search or filter.
- The card catalog covers the series a user is likely to own.
- Pages are quick enough on a phone that a child does not give up waiting (no target set).

## Open questions
- Which series must the catalog cover, and how soon after a new series appears?
- Who keeps the catalog up to date?
